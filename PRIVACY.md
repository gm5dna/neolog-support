# NeoLog Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 9 April 2026

## Overview

NeoLog is a clinical procedure logbook. Your privacy is fundamental to the design of this app.

## Data Collection

NeoLog does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. Specifically:

- **No patient-identifiable data** is ever recorded. The app does not capture patient names, hospital numbers, NHS numbers, dates of birth, or any other direct patient identifiers.
- **No analytics or telemetry.** NeoLog contains no third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, or tracking frameworks.
- **No advertising.** NeoLog contains no advertising and does not share data with advertisers.
- **No server-side storage.** There is no NeoLog server. All data remains on your device.

## Data Stored on Your Device

NeoLog stores the following data locally on your iPhone:

- Procedure log entries (date, procedure type, outcome, clinical setting, gestational age band, and your own learning notes)
- Your practitioner profile (name, grade, unit, GMC/NMC number, BAPM capability self-assessment)
- App preferences (appraisal year start month, export settings)

This data is protected by iOS Data Protection (NSFileProtectionComplete) and your device passcode/biometrics.

## iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your procedure data is stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. This data is governed by Apple's privacy policy and is not accessible to NeoLog or its developer.

## Subscriptions

NeoLog Pro subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not have access to your payment information.

## Exports

When you export your data as CSV or PDF, the files are generated locally on your device. You choose how and where to share them (e.g., AirDrop, email, Files app).

## Data Deletion

You can delete any or all procedure entries at any time within the app. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. If iCloud sync is enabled, you can remove synced data from iCloud via Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at https://github.com/gm5dna/neolog-support/issues.

Any changes to this privacy policy will be posted to this page with an updated date.
