# NeoLog Support

NeoLog is a neonatal procedure logbook for iPhone, designed around the BAPM Neonatal Airway Safety Standard (2024).

## Getting Help

If you have a question, bug report, or feature request, please [open an issue](https://github.com/gm5dna/neolog-support/issues/new).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my data safe?**
NeoLog stores all data locally on your device. No patient-identifiable information is ever recorded. Optional iCloud backup uses your personal iCloud account only. There is no server-side storage, no third-party analytics, and no telemetry.

**What exports are available?**
NeoLog can export your procedure log as CSV (matching the BAPM spreadsheet format) or PDF (with cover page, summary statistics, and procedure tables). Both are suitable for upload to RCPCH ePortfolio (risr/advance), NHS Turas, or NHS appraisal platforms.

**What is NeoLog Pro?**
NeoLog Pro is an annual subscription that unlocks unlimited procedure entries and CSV/PDF exports. Free users can log up to 5 procedures.
